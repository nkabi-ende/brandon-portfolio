Outcomes · Product walkthrough

From fixture to settlement.

Walk through a South African football market using an illustrative cup fixture.

Identify the right event

Match the event to its competition, season and fixture ID. Similar team names or a changed kick-off time shouldn’t create a second market.

Make the terms clear

For a market asking whether the home team will win in regulation time, show the Yes/No outcomes, the closing time in SAST and the rule before participation.

Apply the published criteria

A draw after 90 minutes plus stoppage time resolves to No under this rule, even if the home side goes on to win in extra time.

Record the final state

If the result is late or the feed is inconsistent, the example market stays pending. Once the result is confirmed against the published rule, record the decision and the evidence used.

Sports markets

Outcomes

Product Architect · Remote side project · Aug 2026–present

From South African football fixtures to market resolution and settlement.

The problem

Give customers a clear view of a market and make every fixture, closing time and resolution rule consistent for the team operating it.

What I delivered

I designed mobile Yes/No and market discovery flows, clear local closing times and price-history views. I also defined how football fixtures move through competition mapping, market rules, regulation-time resolution and settlement.

The product decision

I defined regulation time as the settlement boundary for this type of football market. Publishing that rule up front gives customers and operations the same answer when a cup tie goes to extra time.

The result

The October 2026 staging snapshot contained 39 active demo markets. One release cycle passed 41 targeted data-stream tests covering isolation, reconnects and stale data recovery.
